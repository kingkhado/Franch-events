Questions

Frequently asked.

Anything else, WhatsApp us on +27 61 439 1805.

How will I receive the footage?

We compile all the footage into links that we share with the host, who can then send them on to every guest who was at the event.

How long until I get the final product?

Typically 3–5 days after the event, depending on how complex the edit is. We take the time needed to deliver the best quality.

What is included in the 360° booth set-up?
  • A custom overlay paired with your choice of song and design
  • A red or black carpet walkway with elegant stanchions
  • 4 LED bars
Are there extra items I can hire?

Yes. We have 2 LED up-lights at R200 per hour each and a mini bubble machine at R350 per hour. For an extra R300 we can also design a personalised image for the base of the 360° booth.

Where do you operate?

We work mainly in and around Gauteng. For events further afield, get in touch and we’ll let you know what’s possible.
